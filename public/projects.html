Frequently Asked
Questions.

Everything you need to know about our robotics programs, AI studios, school partnerships, hardware kits, and certificate verifications.

What programs and courses does Credible Create offer for schools and colleges?

+

Credible Create provides hands-on experiential learning tracks in Robotics & Cybernetics, Innovation Design and Thinking with AI, Aerospace & Drones, and IoT Engineering. Programs are structured for School students (Class 6 - 12) as well as College / University technical cohorts.

How do schools integrate Bot Barracks robotics labs into their campus?

+

We set up complete turnkey cybernetics and robotics lab infrastructure directly on school campuses. This includes hardware development kits, modular semester-wise curricula, faculty training workshops, and dedicated technical mentors who lead weekly lab sessions.

How can students verify their official cohort completion certificates?

+

Every certificate issued by Credible Create features an encrypted credential ID. Students, parents, and academic institutions can verify credentials 24/7 on our Verify Portal by entering their unique certificate ID code.

Are physical hardware kits included during the hands-on lab sessions?

+

Yes! Every participating student gets direct access to physical development kits—including ESP32 microcontrollers, Arduino boards, Raspberry Pi modules, IR sensor arrays, motor drivers, and 3D-printed chassis components to build real-world physical prototypes.

How can our school or institution schedule a live briefing or campus walkthrough?

+

You can schedule a live 1:1 demo or campus walkthrough anytime by clicking the Join button in the navigation bar or submitting a brief request. Our technical education team will reach out within 24 hours.
