PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE DATE: AUGUST 2026 // VERSION 2.1

01. INTRODUCTION

Credible Create ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and personal data of students, educators, parents, and institutional partners who interact with our robotics training programs, Bot Barracks software, and web applications.

This Privacy Policy outlines how information is collected, stored, processed, and safeguarded when using our services.

02. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect data necessary to deliver high-quality innovation and technology education:

  • Account & Registration Details: Full name, email address, school/institution name, grade level, and contact numbers.
  • Certificate & Enrollment Records: Certificate serial numbers, program completion status, assessment milestones, and issuance dates.
  • Software Diagnostic Logs: Anonymous telemetry, OS platform version, crash logs, and hardware device specs during Bot Barracks simulation sessions.
  • Communication Data: Inquiries submitted via demo booking forms, WhatsApp support channels, or email communications.

03. STUDENT & MINOR PRIVACY SAFEGUARDS

Because our programs serve K-12 students (Class 6-12), we enforce strict student privacy protections:

  • We do NOT sell student data or build behavioral profiles for commercial advertising.
  • Parental or institutional consent is obtained prior to student enrollment in specialized hackathons or certification programs.
  • All student code submissions and simulator data are encrypted at rest and in transit.

04. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

Collected information is strictly utilized to:

  • Authenticate users and issue tamper-proof certificate verification links.
  • Deliver interactive Bot Barracks software updates and MicroPython sandbox features.
  • Coordinate school demo sessions and physical hardware delivery.
  • Ensure platform security, prevent unauthorized access, and fulfill legal compliance obligations.

05. COOKIES & ANALYTICS

We use essential session cookies to maintain your login status and secure admin sessions. We do not employ intrusive tracking cookies across third-party networks.

06. YOUR DATA RIGHTS & CONTACT

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data stored in our system. For data inquiries, please contact our Data Protection Officer at:
